8. KEY FINDINGS
===============

1. The segmentation model achieved its strongest performance on
   Whole Tumor segmentation, with a Dice score of 0.859 on the
   UCSF-PDGM test set.

2. Tumor Core and Enhancing Tumor segmentation achieved Dice scores
   of 0.758 and 0.727 respectively.

3. Molecular marker classification achieved strong test-set
   discrimination for IDH Status (AUC = 0.917) and WHO Grade
   (AUC = 0.976).

4. Classification performance was stable across the five random
   seeds, with IDH AUC ranging from 0.910 to 0.925 and WHO Grade
   AUC ranging from 0.975 to 0.978.

5. The proposed confidence-morphology gate achieved the lowest
   validation loss among the evaluated gate configurations
   (0.7511), outperforming the encoder-only, fixed-gate, and
   confidence-only variants.

6. The model demonstrated cross-dataset generalization, retaining
   meaningful segmentation performance on BraTS 2023 GLI and UTSW
   Glioma without retraining.

7. Classification performance decreased on external datasets,
   particularly for UPENN-GBM IDH classification (AUC = 0.503),
   highlighting the effect of cross-dataset distribution shift.

8. McNemar's exact test showed that the model's higher accuracy
   relative to the baseline was not statistically significant on
   the UCSF-PDGM test set for either IDH Status or WHO Grade.

9. On the training set, the corresponding McNemar tests were
   statistically significant for both IDH Status and WHO Grade.
